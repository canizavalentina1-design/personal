Creamy Tuscan Pasta

A silky, sun-dried tomato pasta with spinach and garlic. Comforting, quick, and deeply satisfying.

Prep 10 minCook 20 minServes 4

Ingredients 6 items

  • 250 g rigatoni
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 120 g sun-dried tomatoes
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • 60 g baby spinach

Method 4 steps

  1. Cook pasta until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water.
  2. Sauté garlic and tomatoes in olive oil until fragrant.
  3. Stir in cream and spinach. Simmer until just wilted.
  4. Toss with pasta, loosening with pasta water. Season and serve.
✦

Chef's tip
Add a splash of pasta water at the end for a glossy, silky sauce.
